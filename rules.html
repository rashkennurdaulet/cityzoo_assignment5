Visitor Safety Guidelines

To ensure a safe and pleasant environment for both guests and animals, please adhere to our core park policies.

1

Do Not Feed the Animals

All species follow strict veterinary-approved diets. Human food causes severe health issues.

2

Stay Behind Barriers

Fences and safety glass protect both you and the animals. Do not cross safety lines or climb fences.

3

No Flash Photography

Bright lights and flashes startle nocturnal creatures and confuse predators in closed exhibits.

4

Keep the Park Clean

Dispose of trash in designated recycling bins. Help us maintain a green eco-friendly zoo environment.

Note: Parents and guardians are strictly responsible for supervising children at all times throughout the park grounds.
